## Common Screening Questions & Answers

### 1. How are you doing today?

> Good morning, Ria. I’m doing well, thank you. I’m glad to be here and looking forward to the conversation.

### 2. Are you interested in this opportunity?

> Yes, absolutely. I’m very interested in this opportunity. The role aligns well with my experience and career goals, and I’m looking forward to learning more about the position, team, and projects.

### 3. Does the resume information look accurate?

> Yes, that’s accurate overall. My relevant experience is approximately 3.5 years, and my total professional experience is also approximately 3.5 years. The rest of the information looks correct.

### 4. Are you open to relocating?

> Yes, I’m open to relocating to Bengaluru for the right opportunity. I’m comfortable with the relocation and would be willing to move based on the requirements of the role.

### 5. What is your earliest joining date? Is your notice period negotiable?

> I currently have a 90-day notice period, and I have not resigned yet. However, the notice period is negotiable, and I would be open to discussing an earlier joining date depending on the requirements of the role and offer timeline.

### 6. Are you holding any offers?

> No, I’m not currently holding any offers from other organizations.

### 7. What is your current CTC and fixed/variable split?

> My current CTC is approximately 10 LPA, and it is entirely fixed with no variable pay component.

### 8. What is your salary increment cycle?

> My current company follows an annual salary increment cycle, which generally takes place once every year.

### 9. What month does the increment usually occur?

> The usual increment cycle is in April. My last salary increment was effective from 1st April 2026.

### 10. What are your CTC expectations?

> I’m looking for a standard increment over my current CTC, but I’m flexible depending on the role, responsibilities, and overall opportunity. I’m open to discussing compensation based on the position and company’s salary structure.

### 11. Are you willing to travel domestically/internationally?

> Yes, I’m comfortable with traveling as required for the role, including both domestic and international travel based on client and project requirements.

### 12. Are you comfortable with the hybrid work model?

> Yes, I’m comfortable with the hybrid work model and am willing to work from the Deloitte office or client location on all working days whenever required based on business or client requirements.

### 13. What attracted you to this role?

> I was attracted to this role because it aligns well with my experience in Java and backend development, and it provides an opportunity to work on challenging projects and grow technically. I’m particularly interested in exposure to large-scale applications, different clients, and greater responsibilities.

### 14. What skills and experience make you a strong fit?

> I have around 3.5 years of experience in Java backend development, primarily working with Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, REST APIs, Spring Data JPA, Hibernate, and PostgreSQL. I also have experience with Angular and security technologies such as Spring Security, OAuth2, JWT, and Keycloak. I believe my backend experience, microservices understanding, problem-solving skills, teamwork, and ability to learn quickly make me a strong fit.

### 15. Is there anything you’d like to know about Deloitte?

> Yes. I’d be interested to know more about the kind of projects and technologies I would be working with, as well as opportunities for technical growth and learning. I’d also like to understand the team structure and expectations for the Senior Consultant role.

### 16. Closing response

> Thank you, Ria, for the opportunity and for taking the time to speak with me. I appreciate the information and look forward to hearing from the team regarding the next steps. Have a great day!
